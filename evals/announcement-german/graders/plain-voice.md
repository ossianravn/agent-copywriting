Judge only the announcement itself, not any note the assistant wrote around it. Judge by what each sentence does, in whatever words.

PASS if the announcement informs in plain statements throughout.

FAIL if it contains any of these: a question that the writer answers straight away; a sentence whose only job is to announce or build up to the next one; a sentence telling the reader how to feel about the move, such as how exciting it is; a closing line that restates the message.

A greeting and a line saying whom to ask are fine.
