Judge only the announcement itself, not any note the assistant wrote around it.

PASS if the first two lines tell the reader that the office is moving, where to (Großer Burstah 31), and when (first day there is Monday 29 March), and if the packing deadline (Thursday 25 March, 16:00) is stated with its date and time.

FAIL if the reader has to read past the first two lines to learn that the office is moving, the new address or the date, or if the packing deadline lacks its date or time.
