Judge only the landing page copy, not any note the assistant wrote around it.

PASS if the copy offers one main action, named for its outcome (starting the free trial), and says that the trial lasts 14 days.

FAIL if several different actions compete, if the action is a bare "Learn more" or "Get started" with no mention of the trial, or if the 14 days are missing.
