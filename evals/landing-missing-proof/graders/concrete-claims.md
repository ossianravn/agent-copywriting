Judge only the landing page copy, not any note the assistant wrote around it.

PASS if the copy describes concretely what the product does, using the facts given: orders imported from a spreadsheet, stops put in order, routes sent to each driver's phone, up to 20 vans.

FAIL if it leans on general promises (words to the effect of effortless, powerful, seamless, save time and money, transform your business) in place of those facts, or leaves out most of them.
