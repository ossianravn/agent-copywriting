Judge only the post itself, not any note the assistant wrote around it. Judge by what each sentence does, in whatever words.

PASS if the post tells what happened in plain statements, in the voice of a person describing their own business.

FAIL if it contains any of these: a question that the writer answers straight away; a one-line paragraph or fragment that adds no information and exists only for effect; a sentence whose only job is to build up to the next one; a statement of what something is not, when nothing suggests the reader believed it; a closing moral, a slogan, or a question added to invite comments; hashtags or arrows used as decoration.

A short sentence that carries a fact or a reservation is fine, also when it stands alone.
