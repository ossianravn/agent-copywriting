Write a LinkedIn post for me about what we learned. My notes:

I run a six-person web agency. Last year we stopped doing free pitches. I was afraid we would lose clients. What happened: in 2024 we pitched for 11 projects and won 3. In 2025 we offered a paid discovery workshop instead, at 8,000 kr. Seven prospects took it and five became clients. We got fewer leads but wasted less time: about 120 hours of unpaid pitch work in 2024, none in 2025. Revenue was roughly flat. I'm not saying it works for everybody.
