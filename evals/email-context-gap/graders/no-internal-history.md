Judge only the email itself, not any note the assistant wrote around it.

PASS if everything in the email is something a customer needs in order to understand the change or act on it.

FAIL if the email includes internal matters: which alternative the team rejected and why, who argued for the decision, the old PDF export, the migration script, the staging test, the number of accounts, or the name of the customer who reported the rounding bug.
