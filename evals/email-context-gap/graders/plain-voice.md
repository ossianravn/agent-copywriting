Judge only the email itself, not any note the assistant wrote around it. Judge by what each sentence does, in whatever words.

PASS if the email informs in plain statements throughout.

FAIL if it contains any of these: a question that the writer answers straight away; a sentence or fragment whose only job is to announce or build up to the next one; a statement of what something is not, when nothing suggests the reader believed it; a sentence telling the reader how to feel about the news, such as how exciting or significant it is; a closing line that restates the message or draws a moral.

A greeting, a thank-you and a line saying where to get help are fine.
